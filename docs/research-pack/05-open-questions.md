# Open Questions

These are the questions I want outside reviewers to challenge.

## Edge Questions

1. Is there any real edge in Polymarket directional taker trading for a retail-speed bot?
2. Are long-dated crypto threshold contracts structurally mispriced often enough to matter?
3. Are rates markets too slow to validate edge in a practical iteration loop?
4. Is the better opportunity maker behavior, not taker behavior?

## Market Selection Questions

1. Which Polymarket categories are most likely to have weak pricing versus informed pricing?
2. Are weather and novelty/event contracts mostly traps rather than opportunities?
3. Should the bot focus only on one narrow class of crypto contracts?

## Modeling Questions

1. Is the crypto model overconfident on first-to-hit and threshold markets?
2. Which signals are genuinely informative versus decorative?
3. Are dvol/funding useful at the horizons being traded?
4. Is microstructure adding signal or just noise?

## Execution Questions

1. Is a taker-only architecture fundamentally doomed after spread and fees?
2. Should the strategy switch to maker quoting / liquidity rewards instead?
3. Is fast exit on crypto more realistic than hold-to-resolution?

## Evaluation Questions

1. What minimum resolved sample size is enough before trusting a lane?
2. Which metrics matter most:
   - realized P&L
   - Brier score
   - calibration
   - hit rate
3. What should be treated as housekeeping and excluded from calibration?

## Help Requested
I want reviewers to tell me:
- which strategies should be shut down immediately
- which one lane is most promising
- what evidence would be sufficient before risking real money
